About Us

Bridging the lab bench and the fish pond.

BioVerix Technologies is an emerging, research-driven initiative focused on developing practical scientific solutions for sustainable aquaculture. We bring together aquaculture science, biotechnology, microbiology, and feed technology to support healthier aquatic production systems.

Our initial focus is laboratory and research-support services — fish feed quality assessment, water quality analysis, and microbiological studies — built for students, researchers, aquaculture professionals, feed developers, institutions, and emerging biotech projects.

Our long-term goal is to develop innovative, scientifically supported technologies for aquaculture health, productivity, and sustainability.
